The daily three

Gentle puzzles, endlessly fresh

Every puzzle is generated backwards from a solved state — so each one is guaranteed solvable, with a known par. The difficulty rotates daily, so no puzzle type is ever permanently “the hard one.”

Switchboard

Rotate wire tiles until every line is routed — one connected network, no loose ends.

Rings

Rotate the concentric rings until every spoke shows a single color, from hub to rim.

Paths

Draw one line through every cell, hitting the numbered waypoints in order.

The streak

Miss a day? The vault forgives.

Your streak grows every day you complete the vault — and Streak Vault is built so one bad day never erases months of effort.

  • 🧊 Streak freezesHold up to two. A freeze quietly covers a missed day so your streak survives without you lifting a finger.
  • 🔧 Repair yesterdayMissed yesterday's vault? Repair it with coins within 48 hours and the chain stays whole.
  • 🏆 AchievementsKeeper of the Flame, Vault Cracker, No Help Needed — bronze, silver, and gold tiers with coin rewards.
  • 📅 The archiveTap any past day on the calendar to replay its vault, just for the joy of it.
